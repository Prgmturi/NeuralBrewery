## Frequently asked questions

1. What is the waterproof rating of this valve?
The motor housing is rated IP65 against ingress. If it is installed permanently outdoors, place it under a roof or in a protective box, to avoid a long exposure to rain.

2. What is the difference between N.C. (normally closed) and N.O. (normally open)?
An N.C. valve is closed without power and opens when powered. An N.O. valve is open without power and closes when powered, blocking the flow of liquids or gases.

3. What is the difference between full port and standard port?
In a full port valve, the inner diameter of the valve is the same as the pipe it connects to. In a standard port valve, the inner diameter of the valve is smaller than the pipe.

4. Can it be used for drinking water?
Yes. The motorized ball valve is made of lead-free stainless steel and is certified NSF/ANSI/CAN 61.

5. For auto-return valves, what is the minimum power-on time to store the energy that closes the valve on a power failure?
For the auto-return to work on a power failure, power the device for about one minute, to charge the capacitor of the 2-wire auto-return motorized ball valves.

6. Can this valve be used with propane?
Yes, our motorized ball valves are suitable for propane.

## Warnings

1. In the 2-wire reverse-polarity and the 3-wire models, the valve is de-energized when fully open or fully closed. Other models may draw a negligible power when fully open, while the 5-wire model draws power when closed, to keep its indicator lights on. See the data sheet for details.

2. The valves shown in this sheet are not designed to be operated by hand. Trying to open or close the valve by hand may damage the valve or cause injury.

3. Only the auto-return models go back to the closed position on a power failure. All other models stay in their current position. If this is a problem, choose an auto-return model.

4. Although the motorized ball valves are rated IP65, which makes them resistant to water splashes, enclose the valve in a protective housing if it is installed permanently outdoors, for a long life.

5. These valves are designed with power-limiting functions, which allow continuous power without risk of overheating. Always install the valve correctly and follow the manufacturer's guidelines, for a safe operation.

## Valve attributes

Operating states:
- Normally closed: the valve is closed when the power is off, and opens when the power is on.
- Normally open: the valve is open when the power is off, and closes when the power is on.
